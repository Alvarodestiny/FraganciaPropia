Yes I Was Madly In Love, But That Was Yesterday · By Kilian

Alternativas baratas a Yes I Was Madly In Love, But That Was Yesterday

Yes I Was Madly In Love, But That Was Yesterday es un perfume unisex de By Kilian de aromas amaderados, dulces y florales. Destacan notas de frambuesa, white tobacco y cuero. La comunidad lo asocia sobre todo a otoño y invierno y a la tarde y la noche, con una presencia intensa. Por precio entra en el rango nicho (normalmente desde unos 150 €).
